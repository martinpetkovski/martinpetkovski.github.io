02 · About the game

You dreamed of flying cars

Tagline

You dreamed of flying cars. You got them. But not without a fight.

Short description

Static Over Midtown is a multiplayer retro-futuristic flying car combat game. Two teams of up to five flying cars battle across a neon-lit pixel-art city of the future, racing to bring down the enemy's main tower.

One line

Team-based flying car combat over a neon pixel-art city of the future.

Long description

The future arrived exactly as the brochures promised: hover-buses drifting between office towers, landmark spires lit up against the night, and a flying car in every garage. Then the city split in two. Red against Blue. Static Over Midtown drops you into the cockpit of a hovering supercar and asks you to take the skyline back, one tower at a time.

Matches pit two teams of up to five cars against each other, from tense one-on-one duels to full five-on-five brawls. Each side defends a main tower and two defense towers that shell anyone who strays too close. The main tower also heals its team, so every push is a gamble: break through the defenses, survive the crossfire, and destroy the enemy's main tower to win.

Every car brings its own set of abilities, from steerable rockets to shields and gravity wells. Cars carry real momentum, so thrust, drift and collisions are weapons too. Empty seats are filled by AI drivers that flank, retreat to heal and pick their moments.

It all runs on Spookee Engine, Return Zero's in-house engine, which lights detailed pixel art with dynamic lights, normal maps, screen-space shadows, bloom and light shafts, so the city glows the way you remember the future.

03 · Key features

Not without a fight

01

Red vs Blue, up to 5v5

Duel one-on-one or bring the whole squad. Matches scale from 1v1 to 5v5, and AI drivers take any seat nobody is sitting in.

02

Take the tower

Each team defends a main tower and two defense towers that fire arcing shots at attackers. Main towers heal nearby allies. Destroy the enemy's main tower to win.

03

Handling with momentum

Thrust builds and releases smoothly and inertia carries you through every turn. Knockback, ramming and boosts make the car itself a weapon.

04

A city of the future

A night-time megacity of glowing office blocks and landmark towers, with sky traffic and border police patrolling the edges of the map.

05

Pixel art, real light

Hand-crafted pixel art lit by an in-house engine: dynamic lights, normal-mapped sprites, screen-space shadows, bloom, light shafts and heat haze.

06

Play your way

Keyboard and mouse or gamepad, with every control rebindable. Fight AI drivers, play local multiplayer or sharpen up in target practice.
