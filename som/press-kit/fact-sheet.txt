SHORT DESCRIPTION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Static Over Midtown is a multiplayer retro-futuristic flying car combat game.
Two teams of up to five flying cars battle across a neon-lit pixel-art city of
the future, racing to bring down the enemy's main tower.


LONG DESCRIPTION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The future arrived exactly as the brochures promised: hover-buses drifting
between office towers, landmark spires lit up against the night, and a flying
car in every garage. Then the city split in two. Red against Blue. Static Over
Midtown drops you into the cockpit of a hovering supercar and asks you to take
the skyline back, one tower at a time.

Matches pit two teams of up to five cars against each other, from tense
one-on-one duels to full five-on-five brawls. Each side defends a main tower
and two defense towers that shell anyone who strays too close. The main tower
also heals its team, so every push is a gamble: break through the defenses,
survive the crossfire, and destroy the enemy's main tower to win.

Every car brings its own set of abilities, from steerable rockets to shields
and gravity wells. Cars carry real momentum, so thrust, drift and collisions
are weapons too. Empty seats are filled by AI drivers that flank, retreat to
heal and pick their moments.

It all runs on Spookee Engine, Return Zero's in-house engine, which lights
detailed pixel art with dynamic lights, normal maps, screen-space shadows,
bloom and light shafts, so the city glows the way you remember the future.


KEY FEATURES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Red vs Blue, up to 5v5: matches scale from 1v1 to 5v5, and AI drivers take
  any empty seat.
- Take the tower: each team defends a main tower and two defense towers that
  fire arcing shots at attackers. Main towers heal nearby allies. Destroy the
  enemy's main tower to win.
- Handling with momentum: thrust builds and releases smoothly, inertia carries
  you through turns, and knockback, ramming and boosts make the car a weapon.
- A city of the future: a night-time megacity of glowing office blocks and
  landmark towers, with sky traffic and border police patrolling the edges of
  the map.
- Pixel art, real light: hand-crafted pixel art lit by an in-house engine with
  dynamic lights, normal-mapped sprites, screen-space shadows, bloom, light
  shafts and heat haze.
- Play your way: keyboard and mouse or gamepad, every control rebindable;
  fight AI drivers, play local multiplayer or practise on targets.
