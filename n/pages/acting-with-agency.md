I think the single biggest superpower people have is the realization that the world around them is completely mutable. Most everything that happens is because a person made it happen.

Look around the room you’re sitting in. Look at everything. Every noun. It almost all exists because a person willed it into existence. Nothing is stopping you from doing the same. 

These people don’t wait for someone else to do something, for someone to teach them, for someone to give them the path, etc. They just go out and find a way to do it. This is acting with agency.
